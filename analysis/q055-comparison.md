# Q55: How is Christ exalted in His sitting at the right hand of the Father?

## Verdict: CONSISTENT

## Summary
BLC Q55 is a near-verbatim modernization of WLC Q54, preserving all substantive theological content about Christ's session at the Father's right hand. The question faithfully exposits the 1689 Confession's teaching on Christ's session and intercession, appropriately expands the Shorter Catechism's summary reference, and maintains strong structural solidarity with the WLC. No omissions, contradictions, or structural alterations are present. The proof texts expand helpfully upon the WLC's citations.

## Shorter Catechism Comparison
**Parallel Question(s):**
> Q. Wherein consisteth Christ's exaltation?
> A. Christ's exaltation consisteth in his rising again from the dead on the third day (1 Cor. 15:4), in ascending up into heaven (Mark 16:19), in sitting at the right hand of God the Father (Eph. 1:20), and in coming to judge the world at the last day (Acts 1:11; 17:31).

- Omissions: None. The Shorter merely lists "sitting at the right hand of God the Father" as one of the four aspects of exaltation. The BLC Q55 properly expands this single phrase into a full exposition, which is the expected role of a Larger Catechism.
- Structural changes: None. The Shorter's four-part structure of exaltation (resurrection, ascension, session, return) is preserved across BLC Q52-Q56, with Q55 taking up the session specifically.
- The Shorter's phrase "sitting at the right hand of God the Father" is preserved verbatim in the BLC's answer (with modernized capitalization).

## 1689 Confession Comparison
**Relevant Section(s):** Chapter 8 ("Of Christ the Mediator"), Paragraph 4:
> "on the third day he arose from the dead, with the same body in which he suffered; with which he also ascended into heaven: and there sitteth at the right hand of his Father, making intercession; and shall return to judge Men and Angels, at the end of the World."

Chapter 8, Paragraph 8:
> "To all those for whom Christ hath obtained eternal redemption, he doth certainly, and effectually apply, and communicate the same; making intercession for them, uniting them to himself by his spirit, revealing unto them, in and by the word, the mystery of salvation; perswading them to believe, and obey; governing their hearts by his word and spirit, and overcoming all their enemies by his Almighty power, and wisdom..."

- Omissions: None. The Confession's treatment of the session is compressed ("sitteth at the right hand of his Father, making intercession") and the BLC appropriately expands this with the full WLC-derived exposition. The Confession's 8.8 elaboration on Christ's mediatorial work — intercession, gathering by the Spirit, overcoming enemies — is all reflected in the BLC's answer.
- Structural changes: None. The Confession's narrative flow (ascension → session/intercession → return) is maintained. The BLC preserves the Confession's connection between Christ's session and His active mediatorial work for His people.
- The BLC's mention of Christ gathering and defending His church, subduing enemies, and furnishing ministers with gifts and graces aligns well with 8.8's description of Christ effectually applying redemption, overcoming enemies, and governing His people.

## Westminster Larger Catechism Comparison
**Parallel Question(s):** WLC Q54:
> Q. 54. How is Christ exalted in his sitting at the right hand of God?
> A. Christ is exalted in his sitting at the right hand of God, in that as God-man he is advanced to the highest favour with God the Father, with all fulness of joy, glory, and power over all things in heaven and earth; and does gather and defend his church, and subdue their enemies; furnisheth his ministers and people with gifts and graces, and maketh intercession for them.

- Solidarity: **STRONG**
- Intentional Baptist divergences: None present. This question involves Christology and soteriology, not baptism, ecclesiology, or covenant theology — no Baptist divergence is expected or found.
- Unexpected divergences: None of substance.
- Key phrases dropped: None. All WLC elements are preserved:
  - "as God-man" → "as the God-man" (article added)
  - "advanced to the highest favour with God the Father" → "advanced to the highest favor with God the Father" (modernized spelling)
  - "with all fulness of joy" → "with all fullness of joy" (modernized spelling)
  - "glory" → retained
  - "power over all things in heaven and earth" → retained
  - "does gather and defend his church" → "gathers and defends His church" (modernized)
  - "subdue their enemies" → "subdues their enemies" (modernized)
  - "furnisheth his ministers and people with gifts and graces" → "provides His ministers and people with gifts and graces" (modernized "furnisheth" → "provides")
  - "maketh intercession for them" → "makes intercession for them" (modernized)

## Detailed Notes
- The BLC's question text reads "right hand of Father" (apparently missing "the" before "Father"), while the answer body correctly says "God the Father." This appears to be a minor typographical issue in the question phrasing — the WLC question reads "right hand of God" while the BLC question has "right hand of the Father." This is not a theological concern but a slight wording variant.
- The BLC restructures the WLC's single flowing sentence into two sentences with distinct proof-text sections, which aids clarity without altering meaning.
- The word "furnisheth" is modernized to "provides" — an appropriate and equivalent substitution.
- The phrase "There, Christ gathers and defends His church" adds the transitional word "There" to connect the session location with Christ's ongoing work — a helpful clarification that reinforces the spatial dimension of Christ's heavenly ministry.

## Scripture Analysis
- **Philippians 2:9** — Cited for "advanced to the highest favor." Matches WLC proof text (note 228). Appropriate: "God also hath highly exalted him."
- **Acts 2:28; Psalm 16:11** — Cited for "fullness of joy." Matches WLC proof texts (note 229). Appropriate: "thou shalt make me full of joy with thy countenance" and "in thy presence is fulness of joy."
- **John 17:5** — Cited for "glory." Matches WLC proof text (note 230). Appropriate: "glorify thou me with thine own self with the glory which I had with thee before the world was."
- **Daniel 7:13-14; Ephesians 1:22; 1 Peter 3:22** — Cited for "power over all things in heaven and earth." Retains WLC proof texts (Eph 1:22, 1 Pet 3:22 from note 231) and adds Daniel 7:13-14, which is a strong and appropriate addition ("there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him").
- **Ephesians 4:10-12; Psalm 110:1; Hebrews 10:12-14; Ezekiel 37:24** — Cited for gathering/defending the church, subduing enemies, and providing gifts and graces. Retains WLC proof texts (Eph 4:10-12, Ps 110:1 from note 232) and adds Hebrews 10:12-14 (Christ's finished sacrifice and ongoing subjugation of enemies) and Ezekiel 37:24 (the Davidic shepherd gathering and ruling God's people) — both strong and appropriate additions.
- **Romans 8:34; 1 John 2:1; Hebrews 7:25** — Cited for "makes intercession for them." Retains WLC proof text (Rom 8:34 from note 233) and adds 1 John 2:1 (Christ as our advocate) and Hebrews 7:25 (Christ "ever liveth to make intercession") — both excellent additions that strengthen the scriptural basis for Christ's intercessory ministry.
- All proof texts are appropriate and well-chosen. The BLC enriches the WLC's citations in several places without any problematic or missing references.
