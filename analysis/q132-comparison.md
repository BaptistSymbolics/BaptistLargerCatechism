# Q132: What is the ninth commandment?

## Verdict: CONSISTENT

## Summary
BLC Q132 simply states the text of the ninth commandment from Exodus 20:16, modernizing "Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour" to "You shall not give false testimony against your neighbor." This is a faithful modernization that preserves the full content. There are no omissions, structural changes, or theological concerns.

## Shorter Catechism Comparison
**Parallel Question(s):**
- Q: "Which is the ninth commandment?"
- A: "The ninth commandment is, Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour (Ex. 20:16)."

- Omissions: None. The BLC preserves the complete commandment text.
- Structural changes: None. Both present the commandment in identical structure.
- The BLC modernizes "Thou shalt not bear false witness" to "You shall not give false testimony," which is a legitimate modern English rendering of the same Hebrew text. "Bear false witness" and "give false testimony" are semantically equivalent.
- The question phrasing changes from "Which is" to "What is" — a minor stylistic modernization with no theological significance.

## 1689 Confession Comparison
**Relevant Section(s):** Chapter 19, Paragraph 2 — "The same Law that was first written in the heart of man, continued to be a perfect rule of Righteousness after the fall; & was delivered by God upon Mount Sinai, in Ten Commandments and written in two Tables; the four first containing our duty towards God, and the other six our duty to man."

The 1689 Confession does not individually exposit each commandment but affirms the perpetual binding nature of the moral law summarized in the Ten Commandments (Chapter 19). The ninth commandment falls within "the other six our duty to man." The BLC's simple statement of the commandment text is fully consistent with the Confession's treatment of the Decalogue as permanently binding moral law.

- Omissions: None applicable (the 1689 does not state individual commandments).
- Structural changes: None.

## Westminster Larger Catechism Comparison

- A: "The ninth commandment is, Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbor."

- Intentional Baptist divergences: None (no baptism, ecclesiology, or covenant theology issues arise in stating the commandment text).
- Unexpected divergences: None.

## Detailed Notes
This is a commandment-stating question with no expositive content — it simply presents the text of the ninth commandment. The BLC, Shorter Catechism, and WLC all handle this identically in structure. The only difference is the BLC's modernization of the language from KJV English ("Thou shalt not bear false witness") to contemporary English ("You shall not give false testimony"), consistent with modern translations such as the NIV. Both phrases translate the same Hebrew (lo ta'aneh bere'akha ed shaqer) with full fidelity.

## Scripture Analysis
The sole proof text is Exodus 20:16, which is the ninth commandment itself. This is the standard and expected citation, matching both the Shorter Catechism and the WLC. No proof text issues arise.
