# Q129: What is the eighth commandment?

## Verdict: CONSISTENT

## Summary
BLC Q129 is a simple recitation of the eighth commandment text, modernizing the traditional "Thou shalt not steal" to "You shall not steal." This is a verbatim commandment-stating question with no theological exposition, perfectly mirroring the Shorter Catechism's parallel question in modernized English. There is nothing to omit, restructure, or contradict.

## Shorter Catechism Comparison
**Parallel Question(s):**
- Q. "Which is the eighth commandment?" A. "The eighth commandment is, Thou shalt not steal (Ex. 20:15)."

- Omissions: None. Both questions simply recite the commandment text.
- Structural changes: None. The BLC uses "What is" rather than "Which is" for the question phrasing, which is stylistic only.
- The BLC modernizes "Thou shalt" to "You shall," consistent with its modernization approach throughout.
- Both cite Exodus 20:15 as the proof text.

## 1689 Confession Comparison
**Relevant Section(s):** Chapter 19 (Of the Law of God), Paragraph 2 — "The same Law that was first written in the heart of man, continued to be a perfect rule of Righteousness after the fall; & was delivered by God upon Mount Sinai, in Ten Commandments and written in two Tables; the four first containing our duty towards God, and the other six our duty to man."

- Omissions: N/A. The 1689 does not recite individual commandments; it affirms the Ten Commandments as a whole as the moral law delivered at Sinai. The BLC's recitation of the eighth commandment is consistent with this framework.
- Structural changes: None.
- The 1689's placement of the last six commandments as "our duty to man" is consistent with the eighth commandment's concern for property rights as a duty toward neighbor.

## Westminster Larger Catechism Comparison
**Parallel Question(s):** WLC Q140: "Which is the eighth commandment?" A. "The eighth commandment is, Thou shalt not steal."

- Solidarity: STRONG
- Intentional Baptist divergences: None (commandment recitation is identical across traditions).
- Unexpected divergences: None.
- Key phrases dropped: None. Both the WLC and BLC simply state the commandment text. The WLC cites Exodus 20:15 in its proof text apparatus, matching the BLC.

## Detailed Notes
This is a purely recitative question with no theological exposition. The BLC, BSC, and WLC all state the eighth commandment identically (allowing for modernization of "Thou shalt" to "You shall"). There is no room for theological divergence in a question of this nature. The substantive theological content regarding the duties required and sins forbidden by the eighth commandment would appear in subsequent questions (paralleling WLC Q141-142 and BSC's questions on what is required/forbidden).

## Scripture Analysis
The sole proof text is Exodus 20:15, which is the universally recognized location of the eighth commandment in the Decalogue. This matches both the BSC and WLC exactly. No proof text concerns.
